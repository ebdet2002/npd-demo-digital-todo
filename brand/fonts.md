# Fonts

### Heading: Space Grotesk
https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Space+Grotesk

A geometric, slightly technical sans with a steady, grounded rhythm — it reads
as deliberate and unhurried, matching the "protected block" feeling Deep mode is
meant to evoke, without tipping into the coldness of a pure grid-based font.

### Body: Inter
https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter

Designed for UI legibility at small sizes, with excellent number/checkbox-label
clarity — the app lives or dies on fast scanning of short task titles, and Inter
is tuned exactly for that job.
